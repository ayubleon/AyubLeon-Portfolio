Overview

Two products, one system.

When I started building out Danadana and BuzzIQ, I didn't want two dev teams rebuilding the same button five different ways. So instead of starting from zero, I used shadcn as the foundation and built out what was missing — tokens, patterns, and components flexible enough to run a fast, high-energy crash game and a denser, data-heavy creator platform without breaking consistency.

You can see both products in more detail in their own case studies below, this is a look at the system underneath.
