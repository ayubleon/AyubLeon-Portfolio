The Accidental Beginning

I got into design by accident. In high school, I built a database interface for my final-year project, and that was the first time I realized I liked figuring out how things should work, not just that they worked. I had more tech ideas than I could explain to people, so I picked up Adobe XD from YouTube to prototype them instead, then taught myself Figma the same way, hours of videos and trial and error until it stuck. That's how it all began, an accidental project that turned into a habit I never stopped chasing.

Off the Clock or When I Close Figma

Outside of work I'm usually messing around with some other form of design. Most nights I'm deep in a YouTube rabbit hole, science stuff, tech reviews, car builds, DIY home projects, whatever pulls me in that week. I take photos of whatever's around me, I game, and I like working with my hands, building things with wood, playing pool. Speaking of pool, if you're not too serious about work sometime, hit me up on iMessage and let's play a round.
