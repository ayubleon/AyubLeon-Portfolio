The Accidental Beginning

I got into design by accident. In high school, I built a database interface for my final-year project, and that was the first time I realized I liked figuring out how things should work, not just that they worked.

I had more tech ideas than I could explain to people, so I picked up Adobe XD from YouTube to prototype them instead, then moved to Figma and kept learning the same way, hours of videos and trial and error until it stuck.

What started as an accidental project became something I never stopped exploring.

Off the Clock or When I Close Figma

Outside of work, I'm usually messing around with some other form of design.

I also tend to fall down rabbit holes. A YouTube video about a car build can turn into an evening of tech reviews, science, DIY projects, or whatever else catches my attention. Gaming and pool have stuck around too. If you're not too serious about work, hit me up and let's play a round sometime.

I've always liked making things, taking them apart, and figuring out how they work. Design is just what ended up becoming my work.
