BuzzIQ

TikTok Shop intelligence platform built to scale creator commerce. IGNITEAMZ, Sep 2025 – 2026.

Sole Product Designer

Client

IGNITEAMZ

Timeline

Sep 2025 – 2026

Platforms

Mobile, tablet, web

Overview

BuzzIQ helps TikTok Shop creators find trending products and the videos driving them, so they know what to sell and what content angles are already working.

IGNITEAMZ needed it built and shipped, with no existing design system and no other designer. My team at PackCommerce took it on, and they brought me in as sole designer based on a quick review of past work, some of it under NDA I couldn't even show them in full. The build had to cover mobile, tablet, and web from day one.

My role

I owned the design end to end, from initial concepts through to a finished, responsive product.

550+

screens across mobile, tablet, and web

3+

months of rework saved by prototyping before build

Built the entire design system from scratch, since there was nothing to build on.

Used shadcn as a base but pushed past the default look with custom variants and assets, so it never read as an off-the-shelf UI kit.

Ran constant check-ins with the team and stakeholders as the product took shape.

The advantage

The advantage that made it work

IGNITEAMZ works directly with TikTok Shop creators, so we had a live feedback loop most projects don't get. We could run surveys and A/B tests with actual users, and creators told us directly what features mattered, what stood out, and what was missing. That fed straight back into the designs.

Challenges

Navigating challenges & quality control

Protecting system integrity

Stakeholders occasionally requested one-off layout tweaks or extra UI elements. I regularly led alignment discussions to demonstrate how arbitrary changes would fragment the design language, offering alternate solutions that solved business goals without compromising scalability.

Developer QA & implementation

My role extended well beyond Figma. I stayed active throughout development, conducting design QA to catch UI drift (such as inconsistent corner radii or spacing specs) and working side-by-side with engineers to match production to spec.

Impact

Business & workflow impact

Everything was analyzed from both sides, what the business needed and what users needed, before a single screen was final.

Once designs were handed off, the dev team flagged anything hard to build so we could solve it before it slowed anyone down.

A prototype was delivered before development started, which helped save an estimated 3+ months of rework.

That back and forth is what let a solo designer keep pace with a moving team.
